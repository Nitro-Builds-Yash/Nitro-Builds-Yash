### 🧠 Multi-Modal AI & Knowledge Architectures

#### 🔹 [OmniChunk](https://github.com/Nitro-Builds-Yash/OmniChunk)
> **Universal Multi-Modal AI Chunking & Relational Structure Extraction Platform**  
> *Understands information before cutting.* Parses documents, audio, tables, and media, anchors captions to figures, reconstructs hierarchical heading ancestry, vetoes cuts that sever relationships, and issues cryptographic decision audit receipts.  
> `Python` `FastAPI` `Docling` `NetworkX` `PyTorch` `Sentence-Transformers` `TailwindCSS` `HTMX`

#### 🔹 [halo](https://github.com/Nitro-Builds-Yash/halo)
> **Production-Grade Self-Corrective Agentic QA Platform**  
> Ingests multi-format documents (research papers, policy briefs, operational manuals) for multi-document reasoning with autonomous hallucination gates, confidence scores, and Reciprocal Rank Fusion.  
> `Python` `LangChain` `Vector DBs` `Agentic RAG` `Self-Correction`

#### 🔹 [voxcode](https://github.com/Nitro-Builds-Yash/voxcode)
> **Voice-Native Software Development Agent**  
> Synthesizes conversational developer intent into AST symbols, runs hybrid BM25 + dense search over codebases, automates plan-and-patch generation, and reviews results with real-time STT/TTS.  
> `Python` `STT / TTS` `AST Parsing` `Hybrid Search` `VS Code Engine`
